The service

AlexAI Resume Help sells one product: the Resume + Cover Letter Pack, for $39, paid once. You send your current resume and one job posting. I send back a rewritten resume and a cover letter written for that posting. A pack covers one posting. For another posting, buy another pack.

The site also has a free keyword check and free guides. Those are provided as they are, for general information. They are not career, legal or hiring advice.

How the pack works

  • You pay through Stripe's checkout page.
  • You email your resume, the job posting and any notes. You can send a PDF, a Word file or plain text.
  • I deliver the resume and the cover letter within 48 hours of the time I have your resume and the job posting. If I need a fact from you, I will email you with a question.
  • One revision is included. Send me your changes once after you have read the pack, and I will update both documents.

AI is part of the process

I draft the documents with an AI model and then read and edit each one myself before sending it to you. The privacy page lists who handles your information.

Only your facts

I write from what you tell me. I do not invent jobs, titles, dates, numbers, grades or skills. If something I need is missing, I ask. I may decline, and refund you, if a request depends on claims that are not true.

You are responsible for the facts in the final documents. Read every line before you send anything to an employer, and tell me if something is wrong.

No guarantees about outcomes

I do not promise interviews, offers, or any result from applicant tracking software. Those depend on employers and other applicants, which I do not control. What I promise is a usable resume and cover letter, built from your facts, delivered as described here.

Payment and refunds

Payment is handled by Stripe. I do not see or store your payment details.

If I cannot deliver a usable pack, you get a full refund. To ask for one, email me from the address you paid with. I will refund the payment through Stripe to your original payment method. How long it takes to show up depends on your bank or card company.

If you tell me I need a fact from you and do not answer, I may not be able to finish the pack. In that case I will tell you, and we can talk about a refund.

Your materials

Your resume and the facts in it remain yours. By sending them to me, you let me use them to make your pack. I delete them 30 days after delivery, as described on the privacy page.
