Privacy

Last updated 21 October 2026

This is a plain-language policy that says what I actually do. It is not legal advice and it is not a certification of any kind.

The short version

  • This website sets no cookies, runs no analytics, shows no ads, and loads nothing from other companies.
  • The three free checks (keyword, bullet and cover letter) work inside your browser. What you paste is not uploaded or stored.
  • If you buy the pack, I use your files only to make your pack and delete them 30 days after I deliver it. The same applies to the cover letter, LinkedIn and interview prep options.
  • I do not sell your information and I do not share it except with the services listed below that I need to do the work.

This website

The site is a set of static pages. It has no accounts, no contact forms, no comments and no sign-up boxes. It uses no cookies and no local storage. It does not load scripts, fonts, images or tracking tools from any other company. The only script that runs on every page assembles my contact email address so it is not sitting in the page source as plain text.

The three check pages run their code in your browser. The text you paste into them is not sent anywhere, and when you close the tab it is gone. You can confirm this with your browser's network tools: pressing the button creates no requests.

The company that hosts the site may keep ordinary server logs, such as the address a request came from and the page asked for. I do not receive those logs and do not use them. When you click the Pay button you leave this site and go to Stripe, whose own privacy policy applies there and which may use cookies.

If you buy the pack

What I receive

  • From Stripe: a record of your payment, which includes the email address you gave, the role you typed at checkout, the amount and the date. I never see your card number or other payment details.
  • From you by email: the resume, job posting and notes you send, and your email address. Please send only what the pack needs. I do not need ID numbers, bank details, your date of birth or a home address, so leave those out.

What I do with it

I use your materials to write your resume and cover letter, to reply to you, and to handle refunds and bookkeeping. I do not use your documents for any other customer, as samples, or in marketing.

Who else handles it

  • Stripe processes the payment. Stripe's privacy policy covers what it does.
  • An email provider carries the messages between us and stores them while they exist.
  • An AI service is used to draft the pack. That means the text of your resume, the posting and your notes is sent to an AI model, which produces a draft. I read and edit every draft before you receive it.

I do not give your materials to anyone else, and I do not post them anywhere.

How long I keep it

I delete your resume, the job posting, your notes and the finished pack from my working files 30 days after delivery, and I delete the email conversations that contain them. I keep a basic order record, meaning the date, the amount and the email address you paid with, so that I can handle refunds and taxes. That record is not deleted at 30 days.

If you want your files deleted sooner, or want to know what I hold, email me and I will do it.

Security

Your files stay in my private email account and a private folder on my computer, not on a shared drive or a public page. No system is perfectly secure, which is another reason to send only what is needed.

Age

This service is for people who are old enough to buy something online and to apply for jobs. If you are under 18, check with a parent or guardian before you buy.

Changes

If I change how I handle your information, I will update this page and the date at the top.
