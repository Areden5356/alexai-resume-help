The free tools and guides

Resume keyword check

Paste your resume and a posting. It lists the posting's repeated terms and shows which ones your resume uses. It is a word count, not an applicant tracking system, and it says so.

Bullet check

Paste the bullets from one job. Five labelled heuristics on each: opening verb, a number or outcome, length, vague words and tense. It never asks you to invent a number.

Cover letter check

Paste a letter and a posting, and type the employer and the role. Nine labelled heuristics: length, names, opening line, overlap with the posting, repeats and passive voice. Each rule explains itself.

Guides

Seventeen plain guides, grouped by job stage, on tailoring a resume, writing bullets from true facts, structuring a cover letter, listing projects, answering reference and gap questions, and following up without being pushy. Every example is labelled as invented.

The tools and guides were drafted with AI and reviewed by a person. Nothing you paste into the tools leaves your browser.
