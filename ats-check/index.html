What this is, and what it is not

This is a keyword overlap check. It picks out the words and short phrases a posting leans on, then tells you which ones your resume contains. That is all it does.

It is not an applicant tracking system, and it does not imitate one. Employers use different software, and some have a person read every application. Nobody outside those systems can tell you how a given resume will be ranked, so treat the result as a prompt to reread your resume, not as a grade.

A missing term is a question, not an instruction. If you really have used Tableau, say so. If you have not, do not add it. A resume that claims skills you cannot show falls apart in the first interview.

How the terms are picked

  • Single words and phrases of two or three words from the posting, never across a comma or a line break.
  • Kept when the posting repeats them, when they are a recognized skill or tool, or when they look like an acronym or a technical name such as SQL or C++.
  • Everyday posting filler such as "team", "strong" and "years" is ignored.
  • Matching ignores capital letters and simple endings, so "managed" matches "managing". Short forms count too: "SEO" matches "search engine optimization".
  • The skill list is short and general. Narrow fields may have terms it does not know. Those are still caught if the posting repeats them.
  • One-letter names such as the language R are not detected.

The format checks read pasted text only. They cannot see columns, tables, text boxes, headers, footers or images. For how to lay a resume out so most software reads it cleanly, see the formatting guide.
